Timestamps (seconds, then text, one per line; click away to apply)

The clip starts Lead-in seconds before your first timestamp so the letters can be seen flying in. So frame 0 of the recording = speech time −lead-in. Line the clip up in your editor accordingly. Recording renders every frame offline at exactly 60 fps and downloads a VP9 WebM. Alpha modes need an editor with the libvpx-vp9 decoder (e.g. Shotcut). Desktop Chrome/Edge.
